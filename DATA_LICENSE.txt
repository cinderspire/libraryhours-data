This feed is a derived database. Records whose id starts with "osm_" (field "o" holds the OSM
object) come from OpenStreetMap; if you use or redistribute this database you must keep this
attribution and offer the derived database under the same licence (ODbL, share-alike).

Records whose id starts with "lh_" were extracted from the public pages of libraryhours24.com
(opening hours as published there) and are provided for the Library Hours Now app.

Opening hours can change without notice. Always confirm with the library.
